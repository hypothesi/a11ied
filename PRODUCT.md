# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Developers and accessibility auditors inspect WCAG findings, reproduce failures,
and decide what to fix. Reports must work offline, with keyboard navigation and
screen readers, on both wide and narrow displays.

## Product purpose

a11ied combines automated checks with observed screen-reader and browser evidence.
Its reports explain findings and show which assessments remain unfinished.

## Voice

Use specific, neutral language. Explain uncertainty and required actions directly.

## Anti-references

Avoid repeated internal error messages, placeholder coverage sections, unreadable
tables, and summaries whose counts cannot be traced to evidence.

## Design principles

Make every failure traceable to evidence and a stable reference. Keep remediation
next to the finding. Show useful page progress before implementation details. Keep
navigation available in long reports. Preserve the distinction between observed
failures, incomplete work, and unavailable capabilities.

## Accessibility and inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Support keyboard use, screen readers, text enlargement, and
narrow viewports. Use text alongside severity colors and standard HTML controls.
